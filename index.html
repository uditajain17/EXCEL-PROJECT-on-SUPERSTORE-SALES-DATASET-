3-Point Mandate to Restore Enterprise Margins

Strategic pillars to eliminate losses and drive sustainable baseline growth
Pillar 1: Repair

Restructure South

  • Institute strict 5% discount caps on low-margin categories in Southern territories.
  • Adjust regional freight and shipping recovery models to prevent tech margin erosion.
Pillar 2: Rationalize

Inventory Governance

  • Establish minimum price floors across all product lines.
  • Phase out or radically reprice Tables (-$7.2k loss), Rubber Bands, and Scissors.
Pillar 3: Growth

Double Down on Core

  • Reallocate marketing focus toward Binders (31.89% margin) and Fellowes PB500.
  • Build cross-selling bundles pairing office supplies with tech sales.
