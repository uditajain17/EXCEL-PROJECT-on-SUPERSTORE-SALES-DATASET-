# EXCEL-PROJECT-on-SUPERSTORE-SALES-DATASET-
EXCEL PROJECT on SUPERSTORE SALES DATASET 
--- ## 💡 Key Business Insights 
1. **Severe Regional Divergence (South Territory Deficit)** * While **Central**, **East**, and **West** regions achieved solid profitability (14.4%–17.3% margin), the **South Region** operated at a severe net loss of **-$14,424.05** (-4.04% margin). * **Telephones and Communication** (-$12,904.99 loss) and **Envelopes** (-$4,321.13 loss) are the primary root causes of margin bleed in the South.
2.  2. **Product Category & Sub-Category Margin Disparities** * **Profit Engines:** `Binders and Binder Accessories` yielded **$59,296.39** in net profit (31.89% margin), followed by `Chairs & Chairmats` (**$48,695.84**). * **Loss Drivers:** `Tables` incurred a global net loss of **-$7,240.07** (-3.74% margin), driven by heavy losses in East (-$10,627.62) and Central (-$8,115.24). * `Office Machines` drove top-line revenue ($318,169.68) but suffered from paper-thin profit margins (**2.77%**).
3.  3. **High Freight & Logistics Overhead** * **Delivery * **Delivery Truck** shipping consumed **$12,137.80** in freight expenses (52.2% of total shipping costs) for heavy items like Tables and Jumbo Drums, severely compressing bottom-line margins compared to **Regular Air** ($149,751.80 net profit). --- ## 🎯 Strategic Action Plan - [ ] **South Region Turnaround:** Renegotiate vendor contracts and freight agreements in the South, focusing on pricing structures for Telephones & Communication. - [ ] **Product Line Restructuring:** Apply minimum margin floors on high-volume low-yield categories (`Office Machines`) and reprice or discontinue unprofitable lines (`Tables`). - [ ] **Resource Allocation:** Focus inventory and marketing investments on high-margin engines like `Binders & Binder Accessories` and `Chairs & Chairmats`. ---
